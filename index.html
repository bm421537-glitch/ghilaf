The story behind GHILAF

GHILAF began with Hajar, inspired by relatives in her family living with ichthyosis, and the difficulties they face with ordinary clothing: fabrics that suit their skin poorly, and treatment-cream stains that are hard to wash out.

From that came GHILAF: clothing that respects the needs of the skin and brings together comfort, practicality and elegance.

We believe comfort is not a luxury. It is a right for everyone.

    Good to know.

    Who is GHILAF for?

    People with sensitive skin, anyone who wants soft, comfortable everyday clothing, and families dressing babies and children.

    What makes the clothes different?

    Seams sit on the outside or lie flat, there are no itchy inner labels, and the fabrics are chosen to be gentle on the skin.

    How do I wash them?

    The garments are designed to be washed at 60°C. We confirm exact care instructions for each fabric and product.
